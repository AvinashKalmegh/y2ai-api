  INTERPRETATION

  NOTE ON INTERPRETATION:
  Granger causality is NOT true causality — it is predictive precedence.
  'DM Granger-causes returns' means: past DM scores improve the prediction
  of future returns beyond what past returns alone predict.
  This is the correct statistical framing for 'signal leads price'.

  COMBINED WITH LEAD-LAG ANALYSIS:
  If Granger test is significant AND lead-lag shows positive lead days,
  this is the strongest possible evidence that DM detects accumulation
  BEFORE price momentum develops.
